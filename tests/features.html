The quiet village by the river

Every morning the baker opens his shop before sunrise, and the whole street smells of fresh bread long before the first bus arrives. Children stop on their way to school to look at the cakes in the window, and the old men who sit by the fountain argue about the weather as if it were politics.

In the afternoon the river slows down, the fishermen fold their nets, and the bells of the church ring for no reason anyone can remember. Visitors who come for a day often stay for a week, because nobody here is ever in a hurry to send them away.
